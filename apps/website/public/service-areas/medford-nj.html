Common questions

Before you get an estimate

Do you replace roofs in Medford?

Yes. Medford is in our core Burlington County service area, including Medford Village, Taunton Lakes, Medford Farms. Our own crews do the work, and availability is confirmed by address.

What does the estimate include?

We look at shingles, decking, flashing, ventilation, gutters, and drainage, then give you one clear scope with materials, price, timing, and warranty coverage before any work begins.

Will my HOA need to approve the new roof?

Some communities require approval of shingle style and color. If yours does, we provide the product details and samples you need for the submission.
