Solar panel installation FAQ

Most All Season Solar residential systems are installed on the roof in 1 to 2 days. Permitting and utility approval typically add several more weeks before the system can be switched on.

Because every panel runs its own Enphase microinverter, a shaded or failed panel does not affect the rest of the system. Central-inverter systems can lose the whole array when one component fails.

Options may be available based on the project, qualifications, and current terms. Compare total payments, fees, escalation terms, and ownership details before choosing an agreement.
