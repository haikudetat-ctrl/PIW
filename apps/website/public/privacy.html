Privacy Policy

Last updated September 1, 2026

Information this site receives

When you request an estimate or contact All Season, the site receives the information you submit, such as your name, email address, phone number, property address, project details, and consent choices. The site also receives ordinary technical request information used to deliver and secure the service.

How information is used

All Season uses submitted information to respond to your request, evaluate the property and project, communicate about next steps, operate the website and estimate workflow, prevent misuse, and meet applicable obligations.

Services that support the site

The website uses service providers for hosting, intake and estimate processing, communications, and property, mapping, address, or imagery services. Information needed for a requested feature may be sent to those providers so the feature can operate.

Google Maps Platform content and address features are governed by the Google Maps/Google Earth Additional Terms of Service. Google explains its information practices in the Google Privacy Policy.

Advertising measurement

Only if you allow Advertising technology, we use Meta Pixel and Conversions API to measure PageView, Lead, QualifiedLead, and AssessmentCompleted events. A Lead is measured in the browser after you confirm both estimate permissions; QualifiedLead is measured after your request is saved; AssessmentCompleted is measured only after a real personalized assessment is available.

For those events, Meta may receive hashed email address and phone number, along with limited browser attribution such as IP address, browser type, and Meta browser identifiers when available. We do not send your property address, property imagery, assessment answers, roof measurements, quote amounts, or package selections to Meta.

Your advertising choice

Advertising is optional. You can reject it without losing access to the estimate flow, or withdraw or change your advertising choice at any time with the Privacy choices control on this site. Changes stop future browser and server-side advertising measurement; information already received by a provider cannot be recalled.

Questions

For questions about this policy or information submitted through the site, call (888) 832-5050 or write to All Season Solar, 28 S New York Rd, Galloway, NJ 08205.
