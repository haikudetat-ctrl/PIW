About All Season Solar

New Jersey roofing crews. Real licenses. No subcontractors.

We're a New Jersey roofing contractor built around one idea: the crew on your roof should work for the company that promised the result. Our in-house electrical and solar expertise means we can also protect the roof when energy work is part of the plan.

Why roofing leads—and both licenses still matter.

A roof replacement is too important to hand off. All Season holds New Jersey home improvement and electrical credentials in-house, so our own team can manage the roofing work and coordinate solar or electrical details without passing responsibility between contractors.

That direct accountability is why we can stand behind 25 years of workmanship coverage—not only a manufacturer's product warranty.
