About All Season Solar

Local crews. Real licenses. No subcontractors.

We're a New Jersey solar and roofing contractor built around one idea: the people who show up on your roof should be the same people who are licensed and accountable for the work.

Why we hold both licenses in-house.

Solar installation touches both your roof and your home's electrical system. Most installers subcontract one side of that work out. All Season Solar's crews hold the NJ Electrical License and the NJ HIC License directly, so the same accountable team handles your roof, your wiring, and your inspection - start to finish.

That's also why we can stand behind a 25-year workmanship guarantee, not just a manufacturer's warranty on the equipment.
