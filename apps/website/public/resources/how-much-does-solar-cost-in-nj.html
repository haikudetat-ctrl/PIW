How much does solar actually cost in NJ?

Updated 2026·4 min read

The honest answer is "it depends" - but the factors it depends on are consistent and worth understanding before you get a quote from anyone.

What drives the price

  • System size. Priced primarily by how much of your household electricity usage you want to offset.
  • Roof complexity. Steep pitches, multiple planes, or older roofing can affect install time and mounting approach.
  • Equipment tier. Enphase microinverters, used on every All Season Solar install, cost more per panel than a central inverter but avoid the single-point-of-failure risk and typically harvest more usable electricity.

Why the upfront number is not the whole story

Cash, loans, leases, and power purchase agreements can produce very different long-term costs. Compare the cash price, financed amount, fees, interest or escalation terms, total payments, ownership, transfer terms, and service responsibilities. A lower first-year payment does not automatically mean a lower total cost.

Getting an accurate number for your home

Because roof shape, shading, and usage vary from house to house, a useful number requires a review of your specific address and goals. That produces a more responsible comparison than a statewide average.
