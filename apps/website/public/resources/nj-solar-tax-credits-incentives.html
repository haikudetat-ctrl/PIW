Resource Guide

NJ solar tax credits and incentives, explained plainly.

Updated 2026·5 min read

Going solar in New Jersey usually involves more than one incentive stacking together. Here's what each one actually does, in plain terms - not a sales pitch, just the mechanics.

The federal credit changed after 2025

Current IRS guidance says the Residential Clean Energy Credit is not available for property placed in service after December 31, 2025. A proposal for a 2026 installation should not automatically count on that federal homeowner credit. If your project crossed calendar years, ask a qualified tax professional how the placed-in-service rule applies to you.

New Jersey's SuSI program

New Jersey's Successor Solar Incentive program supports eligible solar projects. Residential systems generally enter through the Administratively Determined Incentive, with registration and production requirements set by the state. Confirm current eligibility and payment terms with the New Jersey Board of Public Utilities before treating incentive value as guaranteed.

Sales and property tax treatment

New Jersey has historically exempted qualifying solar equipment from state sales tax, and exempted the added value of a solar system from your home's property tax assessment - so going solar doesn't automatically raise your property taxes. Exemption rules can be updated, so it's worth confirming current treatment before you sign an agreement.

Updated August 2, 2026. This is general information, not tax or legal advice. Confirm federal rules with the IRS or a tax professional and state program details with the New Jersey Board of Public Utilities.
